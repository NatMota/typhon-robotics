## Matched control and interpretation

The synthetic control draws independent starts, goals and yaw from declared workspace ranges with fixed seed151509. It matches usable personal examples by category, robot, geometry, controller, tolerances and step budget; it does not copy their pose values. Correction draws start near the goal with incorrect yaw; completed draws start within goal tolerance. Feasibility differences are scenario-distribution effects, not evidence that the robot learned from demonstrations. Sample counts are small. See comparisons.csv, initial_distributions.csv and all failure diagnostics in episodes.jsonl.

## Visual observability limitation

The inherited M1 seven-segment numeral 2 and rectangular box are symmetric under a180° yaw turn. Geometry signatures confirmed this during review; the M1 source comment describing the glyph as asymmetric is incorrect. Studs are not modelled. The privileged controller and evaluator retain directed yaw, but a180°-wrong block can look identical in RGB to a correct one. This does not invalidate the logged physical execution or source-state mapping; it prevents treating these renders as sufficient RGB-only supervision for full directed orientation. Add faithful asymmetric stud or numeral geometry in a separately versioned subsequent dataset before that training claim. M1 originals and this completed batch remain unchanged. See visual_review.json and training_suitability.json.

The 56 frozen pilot pilot artifacts also match their saved baseline hashes. A source bundle, exact package inventory and commands.sh are included with this result.
