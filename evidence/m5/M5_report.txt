RESULT AND INTERPRETATION
The broader adaptation did not establish source-task competence: 0/3 source and 0/3 development-validation successes, with no object contact or supported lift. Prompt-only adaptation also completed none of the unfinished cases. The frozen baseline contacted objects in two validation episodes but achieved no supported lift or completion. The W1000 diagnostic failed without contact or lift. Optional loading was not run because its source-success gate was not met.
Already-completed block arrangements were preserved in both cases by all three models. Original strict scores are F 2/2, C3000 1/2, A3000 2/2. C3000 completed03 preserved the block at its goal. Final gripper was open and released criterion passed. The empty gripper was not fully open at the first stability sample (step 1478), then was open at steps 1480 through 1500; the original stable criterion therefore failed. The brick was not disturbed. Original score remains failure.
The broader model reduced mean end-effector travel from 5.49 to 2.31 m on source cases and from 5.13 to 1.26 m on already-completed cases. It still moved the arm, and neither shorter travel nor leaving the block alone establishes completion recognition or a useful manipulation policy. Shared-head and LoRA changes are evaluated together, so their individual effects are not isolated.
Representative source01 and completed01 contact sheets were inspected: the source block remains at its initial location, and the completed block remains in the target while the arm moves. All 25 rollout videos decode completely. These checks do not claim every frame was human reviewed. The invalid first explanation of the C3000 stability failure was rejected by an assertion and preserved in visual_review_initial_assertion_failure.json.
